01 — About

Who I am.

[ Photo ]

I'm a software engineering student at the University of Ottawa with a growing interest in the intersection of technology and human experience. I believe good design is invisible — it simply works, feels right, and gets out of the way.

Outside of coursework I enjoy exploring visual art, typography, and anything that challenges how we perceive and interact with information.

This portfolio is a living document. It grows each semester as I develop new skills and take on increasingly complex design challenges.

02 — Process

How I work.

01

Understand

I start by identifying who the user is and what problem needs solving — before touching any tool.

02

Explore

Sketching, referencing visual communication principles (scale, hierarchy, contrast, balance), and iterating on structure.

03

Build & Refine

Translating ideas into HTML/CSS/Bootstrap, then testing, adjusting, and validating the result.

I'm currently enrolled in SEG3125 – Analysis and Design of User Interfaces at the University of Ottawa, learning user-centered design, heuristic evaluation, and interface prototyping. Key references include the Nielsen Norman Group and Interaction Design Foundation.
