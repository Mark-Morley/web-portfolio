01 — About & Process

Mark Morley.

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Ottawa. I am passionate about technology, programming, and I enjoy learning new technologies, building personal projects, and expanding my knowledge of software development.

Before starting this course, I worked at Nokia for an intership building internal applications using C# and Blazor applications — which gave me a solid foundation in component-based thinking, collaborating with other team members to ship product to users.

When working on a project, I like to start by understanding the goal and planning the structure before writing any code. I research ideas, look at examples for inspiration, organize the content, and work with clients and team members to ensure a clear structure before devlopement.
